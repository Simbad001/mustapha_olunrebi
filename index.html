Master's thesis · Vibration & reliability

Vibration-based lifetime estimation of CNC toolholders

  • PSD
  • Dirlik method
  • NI-DAQ
  • LabVIEW
  • Python
  • 3-axis accelerometers

Thesis at ESA Eppinger GmbH, a toolholder manufacturer near Stuttgart. I estimated toolholder fatigue life from measured vibration in the frequency domain.

Problem

Acoustic end-of-line checks catch defects late and say nothing about remaining life.

What I did

Designed and built the test setup, recorded 3-axis vibration under operating conditions, and built the PSD-to-damage workflow.

Result

Anomalies and defects were detected more reliably than with conventional acoustic testing.

Software · Fatigue & failure analysis

fasolver: a Python failure-analysis toolkit

  • Python
  • Fatigue & fracture
  • Crack growth
  • Buckling
  • 40 automated tests

A toolkit that ranks likely failure mechanisms from observations and then runs the strength, fracture and crack-growth checks that confirm or rule them out.

Problem

Failure investigations often jump to a cause before the numbers are checked.

What I did

Wrote 18 mechanism cards and the matching calculations, then re-solved twelve documented failures from 1943 to 2018.

Result

77 of 77 checks agree with the published values. Every formula is covered by a test.

Product in development · Condition monitoring

Vibration-based condition monitoring for rotating machinery

  • FFT & PSD
  • Envelope analysis
  • BPFO / BPFI / BSF / FTF
  • Gear mesh & sidebands
  • Python
  • Web app

An independent product I am developing, building on my master's thesis: a web-based tool that turns vibration measurements from spindles, toolholders, bearings and gears into a clear health status for each component. Details on request.

Problem

Vibration data gets recorded, but rarely turned into a decision a maintenance team can act on.

What I did

Designed the signal-processing chain: FFT and PSD, band-pass filtering, Hilbert envelope, bearing and gear fault frequencies, and an automatic report that flags each component.

Result

Faults traced to a physical cause per component, validated on public bearing-fault datasets and real test-bench measurements.
